# 🔒 Private 2-Person Chat, Audio & Video Call

A fast, lightweight, and private communications app designed exclusively for **2 people**, featuring **Live Text Chat**, **High-Definition Audio Calls**, and **Full Video Calls with Screen Sharing**.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- 🔑 **Room Code Pairing**: Both people enter the same code (e.g. `7821` or `SECRET`) to enter a private room.
- 🛡️ **Strictly 2 People**: Maximum 2 people per room code. The 3rd person is automatically blocked.
- 📞 **Audio Calls**: Crystal-clear voice calling with real-time call timer and microphone mute control.
- 📹 **Video Calls**: High-definition video with local picture-in-picture preview, camera toggle, and **Screen Sharing**!
- 🔔 **Built-in Ringtone**: Ringing and ringback chimes synthesized via the Web Audio API (0 audio files needed).
- 🌍 **Works for Friends Far Away**: Built-in secure HTTPS public link (`trycloudflare.com`) enables camera and mic permissions over the internet automatically.
- 📶 **Works Completely Offline**: Also works over local Wi-Fi, Ethernet, or mobile hotspot without internet!
- 💬 **Rich Messaging & File Transfer**: Text messaging, typing indicators, image/file sharing (drag & drop, paste `Ctrl+V`), and chat export.

---

## ⚡ How to Make a Call

1. Both you and your friend enter the **same Room Code** (e.g., `7821`).
2. Once the peer status turns **🟢 Online**:
   - Click the green **"Audio"** button to start a voice call.
   - Click the blue **"Video"** button to start a video call.
3. Your friend's screen will ring with an **Incoming Call** popup with Accept / Decline buttons.
4. During a video call, you can:
   - 🎤 Mute / Unmute your microphone.
   - 📹 Turn your camera on / off.
   - 🖥️ Share your screen with 1 click.
   - 🔴 Hang up anytime.
